# openJiuwen Community Code of Conduct

The openJiuwen community adheres to the Code of Conduct set forth in the open-source community [Contributor Covenant](https://contributor-covenant.org) v3.0. Please refer to [version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct.html).

To report incidents of insult, harassment, or other unacceptable behavior, you may send an email to the [Community Committee](contact@openjiuwen.com) for handling.

## Our Pledge as Contributors

We pledge to keep our community friendly, safe, and fair for everyone.

We pledge to foster an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights, and contributions of every individual, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical appearance, neurodiversity, disability status, biological sex or social gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophical or religious belief, nationality or social origin, socioeconomic status, level of education, or any other identity attribute. All who participate in good faith and abide by this Covenant are entitled to equal rights of participation.

## Encouraged Behaviors

While social norms may differ among us, we all strive to meet this community's expectations for positive behavior. We also understand that, due to differences in culture, background, or native language, others may interpret our words and actions differently from our original intent.

With the above in mind, we pledge to treat one another with care and to follow these shared values as our code of conduct:

1. Respect the **purpose of our community**, its activities, and its modes of assembly
2. Interact with others in a spirit of **goodwill and honesty**
3. Respect **different viewpoints and experiences**
4. Take **responsibility** for our words, actions, and contributions
5. Give and accept **constructive feedback** in an appropriate manner
6. Commit to **making amends** when harm has been caused
7. Engage in other behaviors that benefit the **wellbeing of the community**

## Restricted Behaviors

We agree to restrict the following behaviors within the community. Engaging in these behaviors, threatening to engage in them, or promoting them shall all be regarded as violations of this Code of Conduct.

**Harassment**: Continuing to cross boundaries after they have been clearly expressed, or persisting in unwanted personal attention after being clearly asked to stop.

**Personal attacks**: Making insulting, belittling, or contemptuous remarks toward community members or groups.

**Stereotyping or discrimination**: Judging the character or behavior of others based on identity or traits that cannot be changed.

**Sexualization**: Engaging in intimate behaviors that are generally considered inappropriate within the context or purpose of the community.

**Breaches of confidentiality**: Sharing or exploiting others' personal or private information without permission.

**Harmful conduct**: Committing, inciting, or threatening violence and other harm against any person or group.

Other behaviors that threaten the **wellbeing of the community**.


### Additional Restricted Behaviors

**False identity**: Impersonating another person for any reason, or disguising oneself as another in order to evade supervisory measures.

**Improper attribution**: Failing to correctly indicate the source of contributed content.

**Promotional material**: Sharing marketing or other commercial content in a manner that does not conform to community norms.

**Inappropriate dissemination**: Failing to present in a responsible manner any content that includes, links to, or describes any of the other restricted behaviors.

## Handling and Making Amends for Harm

**[Note: The handling measures and remedies listed below are suggestions based on best practices in the enforcement of a Code of Conduct. If your community already has established enforcement procedures, please ensure that you modify this section to describe your own policies.]**

If community administrators determine, through investigation, that a violation of this Code of Conduct has occurred, they will refer to the following "tiered response" mechanism and, based on the severity of the incident's impact on the individuals involved and the community as a whole, determine the most appropriate remedy for the harm caused. Depending on the severity of the violation, lower-tier measures may be skipped.


1) Warning
   1) **Incident**: A single or recurring violation
   2) **Consequence**: Community administrators will issue a written private warning
   3) **Remedy**: Remedies may include a written private apology, acknowledging one's responsibility, or proactively clarifying how one will act in the future to meet expectations.

2) Temporary restriction of activity
   1) **Incident**: Repeated occurrence of a previously warned violation, or a first occurrence of a slightly more serious violation.
   2) **Consequence**: A written private warning is issued, along with a time-limited cooling-off period, intended to underscore the seriousness of the matter and give the relevant community members time to process and absorb the incident. The cooling-off period may involve restricting participation to specific communication channels, or limiting interaction with specific community members.
   3) **Remedy**: Remedies may include an apology, using the cooling-off period to reflect on one's behavior and its impact, and fully understanding how to re-enter community spaces once the cooling-off period concludes.

3) Temporary suspension

   1) **Incident**: A pattern of repeated violations after multiple warnings from community administrators, or a single serious violation.

   2) **Consequence**: A written private warning is issued, along with the conditions that must be met to restore access. Typically, a temporary suspension is intended to give the suspended individual time to reflect on their behavior and consider possible corrective actions.

   3) **Remedy**: Conditions for remedy include respecting the intent of the suspension, fulfilling the designated conditions for restoring access, and fully recognizing how to reintegrate into the community once the suspension is lifted.

4) Permanent ban

   1) **Incident**: Multiple violations of the Code of Conduct in which other tiered measures have failed to resolve the issue, or a violation so serious that community administrators determine the community's safety cannot be safeguarded should the member continue to participate.

   2) **Consequence**: Revocation of access to all community spaces, tools, and communication channels. In general, permanent bans should be used rarely, must be supported by sufficient and compelling reasons, and should be implemented only as a last resort when other remedial measures have failed to change the behavior.

   3) **Remedy**: In such serious circumstances, no viable remedy is available.

This tiered response mechanism is intended to serve as a guideline and does not limit community administrators from exercising their own discretion and judgment in a manner consistent with the best interests of the community.

## Scope of Application

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, and also applies when an individual officially represents the community in public or other settings. Representing the community includes, but is not limited to: using an official email address; publishing content through official social media accounts; or acting as a designated representative at online or offline events.
